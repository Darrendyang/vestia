# Vestia Roadmap

## Delivered in the current open-source core

- Real wardrobe photos, upload, correction, laundry/archive states, search, filtering, and deletion.
- Deterministic dominant-color recognition with honest unknown attributes.
- Automatic weather through location permissions and Open-Meteo with a labeled fallback.
- Constrained outfit generation, adoption, skip feedback, and recent-outfit restoration.
- Style DNA, wardrobe health, color/style distributions, and utilization insights.
- Purchase-gap analysis with compatible-item evidence and persisted history.
- Real-time GLB digital-human rendering, garment/color switching, and 360° interaction.
- AI-photo-try-on consent and provider-gated flow with no fabricated fallback.
- English documentation, CI, product screenshots, and asset provenance.

## Next priority: production intelligence

- Evaluate and integrate an approved multimodal recognition provider with a representative frozen dataset.
- Add batch screenshot import, better garment segmentation, and user-correction evaluation.
- Implement production authentication, consent history, export, and complete deletion orchestration.
- Add an approved VTON provider and evaluate identity and garment fidelity before release.

## Avatar and 3D research

- Personalized avatar capture with licensed body assets.
- Rigged, collision-aware 3D garments and brand-authored GLB assets.
- Multi-view consistency testing and physical-fit research.

The current GLB avatar and geometric garments are an interactive demo, not personalized reconstruction or fit prediction.

## Marketplace and business

- Official weather and marketplace adapters with explicit capability matrices.
- Product normalization, same-product versus similar-product matching, and offer history.
- CPS attribution, commission-isolated ranking, subscriptions, and entitlement reconciliation.

## Release principle

No feature graduates solely because its page exists. Quality, privacy, licensing, latency, cost, and real end-to-end behavior must pass the corresponding gate.
